What a monthly report holds

Each month a PA reports its commitments and the year's inputs so far, due on the day of the following month that the year's set-up gives. Monthly reports lists the year's months. The file has three tables:

  • Commitments: one row per program with commitments (PA, PrgID, YearMonth, the committed amounts and optional comments).
  • Measure and ProgramCost: the year's inputs so far, as a calculation run takes them. ProgramCost has one row per program with its costs for the year to date; Measure may have no rows.

Spending is read from the inputs (program costs plus the measures' rebates and incentives) and compared with the operating budget. Each report shows the approved budget, the operating budget, spend, paid incentives and committed incentives as separate figures for each program:

  • Paid incentives are the measures' rebates and incentives in the year's inputs so far. They are part of spend.
  • Committed incentives come from the Commitments table: incentives the PA has committed to pay in the future. They are not spend, and they are not comparable with paid incentives, so the reports never set one against the other.

Savings and total system benefit are estimated from the same inputs and are not validated. Fund shifts are made in Budgets, not here.

Filing it

Upload, check, price and confirm, as with budgets and claims. Before the due date you can un-confirm; after it, a correction is a new version with a reason.

The year in one view and the 13th-month report

Each year has a page with every month side by side, and a 13th-month report that sets the year's monthly figures beside its final claims.
