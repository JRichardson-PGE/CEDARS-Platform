Role templates

{% page_help "administration/role_templates" %}

A role template is a named bundle of module permissions, such as a CPUC reviewer or an evaluator. An organization's administrators grant one on the organization's page: it gives the member each permission at once, each grant recorded with the template it came from. Changing a template changes what it grants from then on, never the grants it already made.
