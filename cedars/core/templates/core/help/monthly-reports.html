Each month a PA reports its commitments and the year's inputs so far. Monthly reports lists the year's months and due dates.

First steps

  1. Prepare the file's three tables: Commitments (one row per program), Measure and ProgramCost (the year's inputs so far).
  2. Upload, check, price and confirm, as with budgets and claims.

Spending is compared with the operating budget. Committed incentives are not spending. Savings and total system benefit are estimates, not validated.
