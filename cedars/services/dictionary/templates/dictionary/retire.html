Retire {{ f.name }}

{% page_help "dictionary/retire" %}

A field is retired only after a review of who uses it, who relies on it and whether a CPUC decision, resolution or the Standard Practice Manual still requires it. Retiring makes a new catalog version with the field marked retired from the date you give: it stays in the dictionary and in earlier filings and downloads, uploads that still carry the column are read, and the column leaves the upload templates and schemas on that day. Announce it in a release note before then.
