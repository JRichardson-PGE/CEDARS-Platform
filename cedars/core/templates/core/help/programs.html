First steps

  1. Find a program in the program catalog by name or PrgID, or pick a program year.
  2. Open it to see its attributes, every version, its statewide allocation factors and the studies that evaluate it.
  3. To change it, choose Change on its page. A change starts a new version; the earlier one is kept. A locked attribute needs a CPUC programs approver's decision.
