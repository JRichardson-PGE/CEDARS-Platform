Amounts: {{ s.title }}

{% page_help "budgets/shift_lines" %}

One line per program and year. A negative amount moves money out of that program-year, a positive one into it. Blank lines are ignored. {{ s.organisation.code }}'s lines must add up to zero before the shift can be submitted.

A statewide program's line is the change to the whole statewide budget. The system splits it among the funding PAs by their allocation factors: {{ s.organisation.code }}'s portion counts in its own total, so its other lines must make up for it; each other funding PA is asked where its portion comes from or goes among its own programs when it acknowledges the shift. To move money between two statewide programs, enter a line for each: where their allocation rates differ, each funder's net gap is covered the same way. No line may take a program's operating budget below zero.
