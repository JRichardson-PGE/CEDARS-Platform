You can correct records in place only on an open draft (a version not yet confirmed, or the draft an un-confirm or approved reopen leaves). A confirmed version never changes. You need the module's submit permission.

One record at a time

  1. On the filing's page (a claims period, budget filing or monthly report), choose Correct records.
  2. The page opens on Records with findings, rejected records first, each with the rules that found something. Choose Correct beside one, or find any record by its key or any value.
  3. On the record's page the fields with findings come first, with what was found and how to fix it, then the key and the other fields in sections. The i button beside a field gives its type and definition; a field that takes a value list's codes suggests them as you type. Find a field jumps to any field by part of its name.
  4. Change it and choose Save and check (the bar stays at the bottom of the window). It is checked again at once, with the records it affects, and the page leads back to the filing.
  5. Price the version again before confirming.

Many records at once

Upload a partial correction: a file of only the records that change or are new, in the filing's own format. A Delete table lists records to delete. You see every change before it is applied. Limits gives the most records one correction can change.

Every change is recorded in the audit trail with the record before and after.
