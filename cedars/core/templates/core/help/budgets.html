Budget filings follow the program cycle: an application, then true-up and mid-cycle advice letters. Each filing covers the program years the CPUC set it up for, usually four (2028-2031), sometimes more (an eight-year application) or one. Budgets lists each cycle's filings and due dates.

First steps

  1. Your filing follows the filing specification version its deadline was set up with, linked under "Before you upload" on the upload page: its template, fields, rules, engine release and cost-test set.
  2. Upload one .zip with Measure.csv and ProgramCost.csv, or one Excel workbook with those sheets. CEDARS' or CET's field names both work. Start from the budget filing template, which defines every field.
  3. Check: fix what the findings list and upload again.
  4. Price: once nothing is rejected, CEDARS runs the cost-effectiveness calculation.
  5. Confirm to publish. After the due date, a correction is a new version with a reason.

Learn more

  • Fund shifts: move budget between programs within the cycle. A statewide program's budget changes only as a whole, on its own PrgID (it starts SW_), in a shift made by the lead PA of one of the statewide programs it changes. One shift can move money between statewide programs with different leads: the lead PA of each other program confirms it before it takes effect. The change is split among the funding PAs by their allocation factors, the pages show how much each one covers, and each covers its portion from its own programs. A PA's own ID of a statewide program is never on a fund shift line. No shift may take a program's operating budget below zero.
