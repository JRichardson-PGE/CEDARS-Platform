Five confirmations a year

Each claim year is confirmed five times: after each quarter, then once more with the final annual claims. Claims lists the year's periods and their due dates. The claims template (Excel) lists every table and field.

Filing a quarter

  1. Upload the records that are new or changed since the last confirmation. CEDARS compiles them over the earlier confirmed quarters: a record with the same key as one confirmed earlier replaces it.
  2. Check: every record is checked against the claims rules, and deemed measures against eTRM. A deemed value that differs from eTRM rejects the record, except the net-to-gross fields, which may vary and only warn. Blanks that eTRM sets are filled in. The findings file says how to fix each finding.
  3. Price the year to date, then confirm. Quarters are confirmed in order.

Withdrawing a claim

To withdraw a claim filed earlier in the year, list its ClaimID in the Withdrawal table of the next upload with the reason. Its records stay, marked withdrawn, and are no longer priced or counted.

NMEC true-ups

A NMEC true-up names the claim it adjusts in ParentClaimID, with NMEC_TU_Flag set, and is filed on its revised (metered) 8,760 profile. When priced, it is also priced on the profile its parent claim was priced on: the same profile version, read from the profile library that parent's run used, even when the profile has since been replaced. The difference is shown on the version's page, by program, and on the claim's NMEC chain in Linked claims. It is reported only: the results, ratios and cost tests use each claim's own values.

Personal information

Contacts and sites (PII_Contact, PII_Site) never go in the claims file. They are uploaded separately on the period's page, checked against the claims' ContactIDs and SiteIDs, and kept apart in the PII vault. Uploading or reading them needs the PII permission, which a CPUC data steward approves, and every use is recorded with its reason.

Corrections and the change log

Before a due date you can un-confirm. After it, a correction is a new version: ask to reopen, and the version you correct stays as confirmed. A draft's records can be corrected one by one, or by a partial correction file, without uploading the whole file again (Correcting filed records). Each new version is published with a change letter in the change log. Linked claims shows claims that continue a project across years.
