Work that waits on someone is never assigned by hand: My queue reads it live from each part of CEDARS, for every organization you act for, and an item leaves the queue the moment the work is done. The number beside My queue in the header is how many items wait on you. Items with a due date come first; overdue ones are marked Overdue.

First steps

  1. Open My queue. Each row's link opens the page where the work is done.
  2. If the item is for another organization you belong to, switch to it with Acting for in the header first.
  3. Decide, reply or confirm on that page. Every decision needs a reason where the page asks for one, and every decision is in the audit log.

What waits for whom

  • CPUC approvers: requests to correct filings after their due date (an approved request opens a new version for that organization and period only), and changes to locked program attributes. The Budgets, Claims and Monthly reports pages show how many correction requests wait for each program administrator.
  • PA staff who may confirm filings: filings priced and ready to confirm, and fund shifts to acknowledge for a co-funding PA (a shift takes effect once every co-funder has acknowledged it).
  • Both sides of a filing: comments on a filing waiting for your side's reply.
  • Document managers: documents filed but not yet published, unanswered questions on a document, and responses to an EM&V study's recommendations.
  • CPUC data stewards: requests for PII access, after the organization's administrators have approved them, and a change to the PII rules another steward proposed (Data stewardship).
  • Organization administrators: access requests to their organization.
  • A second CPUC system administrator: a draft set of CPUC goal decisions, which the administrator who loaded it cannot confirm.

Publication holds

Before a confirmed filing's records reach the public dashboards and record downloads, every text column is scanned for what may be personal information. A filing with a hit is held, and the public data keep what was published before. The filing's organization sees its own holds on Publication holds. If the values the scan found are not personal information, one of the organization's confirmers certifies that, with the reason, and the filing is published; the certification is audited and the CPUC system administrators are told. Otherwise the organization corrects the filing. CPUC system administrators see every hold and can release one or scan it again after changing the scan settings.
