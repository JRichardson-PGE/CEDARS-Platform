Each claim year is confirmed five times: after each quarter, then with the final annual claims. Claims lists the periods and due dates.

First steps

  1. Download the claims template (Excel) for the tables and fields.
  2. Upload only the records new or changed since the last confirmation; CEDARS compiles them with the earlier quarters.
  3. Check: records are checked against the claims rules and deemed measures against eTRM. Fix and upload again.
  4. Price the year to date, then confirm. Quarters are confirmed in order.

Contacts and sites never go in the claims file: upload them separately on the period's page (needs the PII permission). To withdraw a claim, list its ClaimID in the next upload's Withdrawal table.

Statewide programs

A statewide program is claimed only by its lead PA, which its program page names. The lead PA files these claims in the statewide confirmations, due before the PAs' own: two weeks before a quarter's claim and a month before the annual claim, unless the CPUC sets other dates. They hold only the statewide programs it leads, and are priced with the statewide allocation, so each record's results are credited to the PAs that fund the program.

Once the lead PA confirms, each funding PA sees its share on the year's statewide claims credited page, linked under each year on Claims, before it files its own claim. Its year's totals, such as the 13th-month report, count that share. Don't put statewide programs in your own claims: those records are rejected.
