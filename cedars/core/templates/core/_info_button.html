{% comment %}An "i" button that shows a field's hint (django/forms/field.html, help_as_info). A native popover: it opens with a click, a tap, Enter or Space and closes with Escape or a click elsewhere, without any script (app.js adds opening on hover, and places it under the button). It stays open until dismissed, and the pointer can move onto it (WCAG 1.4.13). The hint's id is the one the control's aria-describedby names.{% endcomment %}
