California Energy Data and Reporting System

{% page_help "data/front" %}

CEDARS is where California's CPUC-regulated energy-efficiency program administrators (the investor-owned utilities, regional energy networks and community choice aggregators) file their program budgets, their monthly and quarterly inputs of what the programs achieved, and where stakeholders and the CPUC review program progress and post evaluation data. About CEDARS lists what the platform holds.
