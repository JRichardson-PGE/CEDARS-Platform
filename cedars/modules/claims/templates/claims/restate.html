How the change is split

When you choose an ACC other than the filed one, two runs are made on the release you choose, with today's program registry and the claim year's allocation version and cost-test set: one on the filed {{ calc.acc }} ACC and one on your ACC. The first shows the effect of the engine and the programs' settings; the difference between the two is the effect of the ACC alone. On the filed ACC, one run is made and the whole change is the program and engine effect.
