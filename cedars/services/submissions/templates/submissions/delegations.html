Approver delegates

{% page_help "submissions/delegations" %}

An approver names a delegate to decide for them for a set of days, for example while on leave: correction requests after a due date and, for programs, change requests. The delegate decides in the approver's name, and each decision records that it was made as a delegate. Ending a delegation early stops it at once; decisions already made stay as made.
