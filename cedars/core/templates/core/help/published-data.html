Everything published is open to everyone, with no account needed. Only confirmed filings are published.

First steps

  1. Open the portfolio dashboard, choose the years, metric and view at the top, then Apply. The page's address keeps your choices, so a copied link shows the same numbers.
  2. Select a row to drill down (PA, sector, program, measure). What is left out is always stated on the page.
  3. Download the rows you see, or the records behind them, as CSV, Excel or Parquet.

Learn more

  • Record downloads: the published measure-level records, with the columns you choose.
  • Saved views: save, share or ask to publish a dashboard or records view (signed in).
  • Document library: PA documents, CPUC guidance, DEER resources and EM&V studies.
  • Data dictionary and its change log: every field and the name CET used for it.
  • Validation rules: what each filing is checked against.
  • CPUC-mandated changes: decisions that change CEDARS' data or calculations.
  • Metric catalog (CSV) and the open data API at /api/v1/data/ (API description).
