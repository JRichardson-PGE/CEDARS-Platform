The portfolio dashboard

The portfolio dashboard shows what the PAs forecast in their budget filings beside what they claimed, from confirmed filings only. Its pages are Overview, Goals, Total system benefit, Program detail, Measure detail, Budget and spending, Cost effectiveness, Energy savings and Environmental impact.

  • Choose the view with the controls at the top: the years, the metric, view by (PA, PA type, segment, sector, implementer type or measure use category), gross or net, first year or lifecycle, and which series to show. Then choose Apply. The page's address holds the whole view, so a copied link shows the same numbers.
  • Filters narrow the rows (a PA, all RENs, a sector, a program). Each applied filter is listed with a link that takes it off again.
  • Drill down from any row: a PA's row opens its sectors, a sector's its programs, a program's its measures.
  • What is left out is always stated on the page: by default pension and benefits programs and codes and standards (and, on the Goals page, Energy Savings Assistance). You can include them.
  • Download the rows you see as CSV, Excel or Parquet, one file per series. A download is the page's own query, so its totals match the page.

Every ratio is summed benefits divided by summed costs, never an average of ratios. Each number's definition is in the metric catalog (CSV).

Record downloads

Record downloads gives the published measure-level records for every PA in one file. Choose claims or a filing, the years and filters, then the columns (each defined in the data dictionary), and download. A large download is made in the background: sign in and CEDARS tells you when it is ready.

Saved views

Signed in, you can save any dashboard page or the records page with its choices (years, filters, metric and, for records, the columns) under a name, from the Saved views part at the bottom of the page. A view keeps the choices, not the numbers, so it always opens on the data published now. Saved views lists your views, the ones shared with you and the ones published for everyone.

  • Share a view with people by their CEDARS account's email address (they are told), or with the members of an organization you belong to. They can open it and save a copy of their own; only you change it.
  • Publish: ask the CPUC to publish a view, and a CPUC system administrator decides. A published view is open to everyone, signed in or not, and does not change until it is unpublished.
  • Your default columns: on the records page, make the columns you chose your default, and the page starts from them instead of the key columns whenever a link names none.

The open data API

Programs can read the same published data as JSON, with no account, from the open data API at /api/v1/data/. It has the metric catalog (metrics), the dimensions, filters and exclusions (dimensions), sums of any metrics by any dimensions (query, the dashboards' own query and default exclusions) and the measure-level records with the columns you choose (columns and records, the record downloads' own query, nothing left out unless asked). Rows come a page at a time with a link to the next page; the most a page holds is on the limits page. A dashboard's query string gives the same numbers in the API, and every answer is stamped like a download: the release, its as-of time, the filings read, the filters and what was left out. Only fields the data dictionary marks public are served. The API description (OpenAPI) lists every parameter.

Documents

The document library holds PA documents, CPUC staff guidance, DEER resources, program files and EM&V studies, with their earlier versions. Search by words, or filter by collection, type, PA, program and year. Each document has a permanent link. Signed in, you can follow a document or a study to be told when it changes, and comment when a draft is open for comment.

The data dictionary and the rules

The data dictionary defines every field CEDARS takes or produces, with its unit and the name CET used for it (both names are accepted on upload). The validation rules list what each filing is checked against, rule by rule, with the versions in force; you can compare two versions of a rule set.

Fields are never deleted from the dictionary. A field no longer needed is retired after a review of who uses it, who relies on it and whether a CPUC decision still requires it: it stays in the dictionary marked retired, with the reviews, and leaves the upload templates from its retirement date (uploads that still have the column are read, and the column ignored). The dictionary's change log lists what each version changed, field by field, with every published version of the value lists and rule sets. The register of CPUC-mandated changes beside it lists each decision or resolution that changes CEDARS' data or calculations, what it requires, when it takes effect, how far it is implemented and where CEDARS carries it.
