Factors are kept to 4 decimal places. Each weighted factor is PercentElectric × electric + (1 − PercentElectric) × gas, rounded; where the rounded values did not add up to 1.0000, the PA whose value was closest to rounding the other way was moved by 0.0001 first. Versions loaded before 6 November 2026 kept the weighted factors their file gave, and were not held to these totals on upload.
