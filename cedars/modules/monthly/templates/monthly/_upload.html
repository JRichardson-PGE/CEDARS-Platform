What the file holds

Three tables: Commitments, and the year's BCA inputs so far, Measure and ProgramCost, as a calculation run takes them. The monthly report template has them all.

Commitments

One row per program with commitments, with the columns PA, PrgID, YearMonth ({{ deadline.period.year }}{{ deadline.period.number|stringformat:"02d" }}), the committed fields CEDARS' monthly reports have today and an optional Comments:

Measure and ProgramCost

ProgramCost rows for {{ deadline.period.year }}, one per program, with its costs for the year to date; measures claimed from {{ deadline.period.year }}Q1 up to this month's quarter. Measure may have no rows.

Each program's spend is read from them, as Budgets counts a budget: the ProgramCost fields below plus its measures' rebates and incentives (NumUnits times {% for f in incentive_fields %}{{ f }}{% if not forloop.last %}{% if forloop.revcounter == 2 %} and {% else %}, {% endif %}{% endif %}{% endfor %}). Those rebates and incentives are the paid incentives, shown on their own as well as in spend. The Commitments table's committed incentives are incentives to be paid in the future: not spend, and not compared with the paid ones. OnBillFinancing is shown apart, as in Budgets. The month's spend is the change in the year-to-date spend since last month's report.
