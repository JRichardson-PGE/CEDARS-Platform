Set up a claim year

{% page_help "claims/year_new" %}

A claim year has five confirmations, each with its own due date: the four quarters, then the final annual claims (due the following spring). Its claims are priced on the ACC chosen here, with statewide programs allocated by the allocation version chosen (or the most recent confirmed one with factors for the year). Statewide programs' lead PAs file their claims first, in five statewide confirmations, each due before the PAs' own so the funding PAs see their shares before they file.
