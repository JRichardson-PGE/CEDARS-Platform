When records can be corrected one by one

A filing's records are corrected in place only on an open draft: a version not yet confirmed, or the new draft an un-confirm (before the due date) or an approved reopen (after it) leaves. A confirmed version never changes; its correction is the next version, which starts with the records it replaces. You need the module's submit permission for the filing's organization.

From the filing's page, choose Correct records. Claims are corrected this way now; budget filings and monthly reports still take a corrected file.

Finding and changing a record

  1. Find the record by its key (a ClaimID, a PrgID, a SiteID) or any of its values, in one table or every table.
  2. Open it: its fields are on a form, with what its last check found beside the fields concerned.
  3. Save and check. The record is checked again at once, together with the records whose checks it can affect: those with the same key, those that refer to it (a claim's other records, the measures of a program whose cost record changed, the records at a site), and the claims rule set's checks on a changed Measure or ProgramCost record. Every other record keeps its findings. The page says whether anything is still rejected.

Add a record from Add a record, and delete one from its form. The version's file is rewritten as a new file (with its own SHA-256) each time, and its pricing is void: price it again before confirming. Check again on the filing's page always checks every record.

Claims filed in an earlier confirmation

A quarter's upload holds the records new or changed since the last confirmation, and the search finds the year's earlier records too. Correcting one adds the corrected record to this version, where it stands over the earlier one; its key stays. A claim filed earlier is taken out of the year by naming it in the Withdrawal table, never by deleting it. ContactClaim, InfrastructureCost and ClaimSite records are one set per claim: correcting one of a claim's earlier records brings the claim's other records of that table into this version too.

A partial correction

For many records at once, upload a file of only the records that change or are new, in the filing's own format: a .zip of CSV files named after their tables, an Excel workbook with a sheet per table, or one table's CSV file named after it (Measure.csv).

  • Records are matched on their key: a record already in the version is changed, any other is added.
  • A column the file leaves out keeps each record's value; a column with a blank cell makes that value blank.
  • To delete records, add a table named Delete with a Table column and the key columns of each record to delete (for example Table = Measure, ClaimID = PGE-2028-0042).
  • One partial correction changes a limited number of records (Limits gives it); a larger one is a new upload of the whole file.

You see every change, field by field, before anything is changed, and can download them as CSV. Apply it, and it is checked the same way as a single record. It applies only to the records as they stood when you previewed it.

What is recorded

Every record changed, added or deleted is an event in the audit trail, with the record before and after, who made the change, the optional reason, and how: the record editor, or a partial correction with its file's name and SHA-256. When a corrected version is published, its change letter and the notice to followers say how many records were corrected one by one.
