Budget filings follow the four-year cycle: a business plan application, then true-up (TUAL) and mid-cycle (MCAL) advice letters. Each filing is uploaded, checked, priced and confirmed; every confirmed version is kept and can be compared with any other. A correction is a new version, published with a change letter in the change log.
