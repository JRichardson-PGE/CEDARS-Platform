A fund shift moves dollars between programs within a filing cycle, across its program years if need be. It is not part of a budget filing and has no cost-effectiveness run. Its amounts must add up to zero. The approved budget never changes; the operating budget does.

A statewide program's budget changes only as a whole, on its own PrgID (it starts SW_), in a shift made by its lead PA. Every funding PA's portion changes by its allocation factor, and each covers its portion from its own programs; the shift takes effect once all have.
