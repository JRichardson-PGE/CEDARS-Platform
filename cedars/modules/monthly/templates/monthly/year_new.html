Set up a program year's monthly reports

{% page_help "monthly/year_new" %}

This puts the whole program year on the filing calendar: thirteen due dates, one for each month's report and a final one for the 13th-month report, due with the year's annual claim. Each month's report opens when its month ends; the 13th-month report, generated from each PA's confirmed claims, opens when the year ends. A due date can then be moved on the filing calendar. The year-to-date BCA inputs are priced on the ACC chosen here for the estimates, with statewide programs allocated by the allocation version chosen (or the most recent confirmed one).
