Each month a program administrator reports its commitments and the year's BCA inputs so far. Spend is read from the BCA inputs (program costs plus paid incentives, the measures' rebates and incentives) and compared with the operating budget (the approved budget plus fund shifts in effect); committed incentives, to be paid in the future, are reported apart from spend; and the BCA inputs give estimated savings and total system benefit, which are unvalidated. Fund shifts are made in Budgets.
