Each claim year is confirmed five times: after each quarter, then once more with the final annual claims. A quarter's upload holds the records that are new or changed since the last confirmation; it is compiled over the earlier confirmed quarters, checked, priced for the year to date and confirmed. Confirmed claims, their inputs and results are open to every signed-in user. After a due date a correction is a new version, published with a change letter in the change log.
