A fund shift moves dollars between programs within a filing cycle, across its program years if need be. It is not part of a budget filing and has no cost-effectiveness run. Its amounts must add up to zero. A shift that touches a statewide program takes effect once every other PA funding that program has acknowledged it. The approved budget never changes; the operating budget does.
