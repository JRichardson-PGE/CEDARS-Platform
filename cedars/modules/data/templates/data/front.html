California Energy Data and Reporting System

{% page_help "data/front" %}

CEDARS is where California's CPUC-regulated energy-efficiency program administrators (the investor-owned utilities, regional energy networks and community choice aggregators) report on their programs, and where stakeholders and the CPUC follow them. About CEDARS lists what the platform holds.

  • Budgets. Program administrators file their program budgets for each program cycle.
  • Progress. They report what their programs spent and achieved, in monthly and quarterly filings.
  • Review. Stakeholders and the CPUC review program progress and post evaluation data.
