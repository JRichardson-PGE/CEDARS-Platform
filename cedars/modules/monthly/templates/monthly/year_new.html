This adds twelve reports to the filing calendar, one per month. Each opens when its month ends and is due on the day chosen here of the following month (a month's due date can then be moved on the filing calendar). The year-to-date BCA inputs are priced on the ACC chosen here for the estimates, with statewide programs allocated by the allocation version chosen (or the most recent confirmed one).
