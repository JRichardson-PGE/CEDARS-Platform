The document library holds PA documents, CPUC staff guidance, DEER resources, program files and EM&V documents. Published documents are open to everyone; drafts are seen only by the people who manage them. A published version never changes: a revision is a new version, and every earlier version stays listed and downloadable.

First steps

  1. File a document in a collection you manage: choose the collection, then its type, the attributes it is filed with and the files. It starts unpublished; publish it from its page when it is ready.
  2. Replace its files by adding a version. An EM&V deliverable (a workplan or study results) moves through its stages (drafts, comments, final, PA responses) as versions of one document.
  3. While a version's comment period is open, anyone signed in can comment or ask a question. The document's managers open comment periods and respond; comments and responses are public, with their author's name and organization.
  4. Follow a collection or a value documents are filed with to hear when a matching document is published or opens for comment.

Evaluation studies

  • Evaluation studies is the EM&V study register: each study's facts, the programs and program years it evaluates, and its documents. Published details are public; contacts are not.
  • Work orders are Energy Division's contract work orders; studies and documents are filed under them.
  • A PA responds to a study's recommendations as a version of the deliverable at the stage PA response.
  • The aging review lists what each study manager owes, and Study budgets compiles the register's budget tables.

Learn more

  • Every stored PDF and workbook is checked for accessibility as it is stored, and the uploader is told what was found. The accessibility guide says how to report a barrier.
  • Document value lists: the types and attributes a document can be filed with.
  • Reviewing and approving: documents and questions waiting in My queue.
