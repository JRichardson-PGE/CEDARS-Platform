CEDARS is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, as California requires of state agency sites, and every page is checked with automated tests in both the light and the dark color scheme.

Keyboard

  • Every control can be reached with Tab and Shift+Tab, and the focus is always shown by an outline.
  • Skip to content is the first stop on every page.
  • The header's menus (Filings, Calculations, Reference, Administration) open with Enter or Space and close with Escape.
  • Filters and view controls change nothing until you choose Apply, so moving through them never reloads the page.
  • In the tour, Next and Back move (so do the Left and Right arrow keys) and Escape leaves.

Screen readers

  • Each page has one main heading, and its sections have headings in order. Tables have captions and header cells.
  • Status is given in words, never by color alone (for example "Rejected", "Due in 2 weeks").
  • Messages that appear as a page updates (a run's progress, a saved change) are announced.
  • Every chart has a table with the same numbers beside it.

Zoom and small screens

Pages reflow to one column at 320 pixels wide and at 200% zoom with nothing cut off; wide tables scroll inside their own box. The header's links fold behind a Menu button on narrow screens.

Without scripts

Pages are made on the server and work without JavaScript. Scripts only add conveniences, such as the guided tour (its steps are also on one page).

Report a barrier

If something cannot be used with your assistive technology, see getting support: a barrier is a defect, and we fix it.
